K-Means Clustering

Click the canvas to place points, pick k, then step through the algorithm to watch clusters form in real time.

Ready
Add points by clicking the canvas, or generate random ones. Then initialize centroids to begin.

How it works

  1. Choose k — decide how many clusters you expect.
  2. Initialize — place k centroids (diamonds) at random data-point positions.
  3. Assign — color each point by its nearest centroid.
  4. Move — shift each centroid to the mean of its assigned points.
  5. Repeat steps 3–4 until assignments stop changing (convergence).
